## Full description
**A new code drops every day. Can you crack it?**

CODEBREAK is a daily puzzle game: a secret 4-digit code, 8 tries, and your
brain. After every guess you get a hint — green means the right digit in the
right place, amber means the right digit in the wrong place. Use logic,
narrow it down, and crack the code before you run out of tries.

**Why you'll be back tomorrow:**
- 🧠 One fresh puzzle every day — the same code for everyone on Earth
- 🔥 Streaks — crack codes on consecutive days to build your streak
- 📊 Stats — track your games, win rate, and best streak
- 📤 Share your result as an emoji grid and challenge your friends
- 🌍 No language needed — numbers work in every country
- 📴 100% offline — play on the bus, on a plane, anywhere

No accounts. No ads in your face. Just you versus the code.
